# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Rules/data truth
- Accept official Kaggle competition rules.
- Inventory every 2027 file and column.
- Build schema adapter; never guess column names.

## Phase 1 — EDA
- Player/drill/position/cohort coverage.
- Missingness and tracking quality.
- Coordinate/frame-rate verification.

## Phase 2 — Fingerprints
- Speed creation, braking, redirection, re-acceleration, curvature, jerk, asymmetry, path efficiency.
- Normalize within position and drill.

## Phase 3 — Translation
- Link Combine fingerprint to analogous NFL movement contexts.
- Player/group-held-out validation.
- Cohort/time split where possible.

## Phase 4 — Hidden Talent
- Expected NFL movement/performance conditional on Combine fingerprint.
- Residual-based Hidden Gem and Bust Risk signals.
- Calibrated uncertainty.

## Phase 5 — Scout product
- Position-specific player cards.
- 3–5 case studies with trajectories and explanations.
- Avoid causal claims unsupported by design.

## Phase 6 — Submission
- Reproducible Kaggle notebook.
- <= competition writeup limits after verifying 2027 rules.
- Adversarial leakage/reproducibility audit.
